Updated ABOUT BIO:

Hello, I am Joan.

Somatic educator, artist, and founder of Ma Somatrix

I received my training in somatic education and sexology from Pacific Center of Somatic Sexology, with continued mentorship with Ellen Heed, PhD. and Zen Shiatsu Chicago.

My approach bridges an East-meets-West sensibility, rooted in an eclectic set of contemporary somatic methods and ancient  classical systems of medicine, philosophy, and internal arts. This approach lends a panoramic field for offering deeply integrated and individualized whole-person care options, so that a client gets a direct lived experience of being in their body.

I come into this work from the arts. Transitioning out of the fine art world since 2016 to teach Butoh across Mexico, the US, Germany, and Japan. Butoh emerged in Japan as a somatic practice of resistance following the post-war atomic bomb trauma. Butoh has offered me a language vast enough to integrate the light and shadow dimensions of being alive. It dances between pain and pleasure, life and death. It embraces otherness, ambiguity, and fluid ways of being gendered and sexual. It welcomes the pain, pleasure, terror, beauty, innocence, grief and joy of the human narrative. Lending itself to its everlasting alchemy.

In time, I’ve gravitated toward Butoh pioneer Minako Seki's evolution of this art form into a more holistic approach to working with the moving body. This begins with how we see with our eyes, how we nourish, and how we care for the body-mind before and after the stage. Happening within a comprehensive lens that integrates meditation, macrobiotic nutrition, Japanese detoxification methods, active imagination, and dance, seeing dance as an expression of living fully. This formation continues to fertilize the ground for my approach and perspective on health and human potential.

After more than a decade of personal and professional immersion, I discover the body as a matrix of wisdom. Where seemingly disparate threads are exquisitely braided into a coherent web. Its threads express through a person's nervous system, authentic creativity, sexuality, pelvic health, scar tissue, biochemistry, biomechanics, and psycho-spiritual energetic field.

At Ma Somatrix, I specialize in supporting people who value self-responsibility, depth, and precision in forging a meaningful relationship with their body.

I enable this process through collaborative explorations that teach them how to integrate body literacy, embodied boundaries, and pelvic and ancestral nutrition care practices into their daily lives.

When these skills are embodied in one's voice and being —trust and intimacy are empowered. Out of this space, the soft power of erotic aliveness emerges to reclaim being fully turned on by life.

Allow me to accompany you through this terrain.

"I'm devoted to bridging the gap between what people think they know about their body and what they actually feel, live, and Know as the pulsing wisdom that lives within their cells, organs, and flesh."
