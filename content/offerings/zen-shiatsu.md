- title: On pressure
  paragraphs:
  - Pressure will be as deep or as gentle as is right for you, and it can shift from moment to moment. Shiatsu is a collaborative practice. You may notice that pressure and attention bring stored tension to the surface, and follow-up stretches help it move through and out.
- title: On pain
  paragraphs:
  - 'There are two kinds of pain: clean pain, which is supportive, and dirty pain, which is not necessary. What we are going for is hurts so good. When we access and move something that has been stored for a long while, you may feel it on its way out, and that is normal. You are standing at your resilient edge of resistance.'
  - What we are never going for is hurts so bad, the kind where your body tenses to protect itself. Even when sensation edges toward pain, it should feel like something you can lean into and relax around, never something you are bracing against. If anything I do feels like too much, please say so, and I will shift my approach right away. This is not a no-pain-no-gain experience.
right:
- title: The abdomen
  paragraphs:
  - A treatment typically opens and closes with the abdomen. Every meridian line runs through the core, so it acts as a window into how your system is doing as a whole. I know this can be a vulnerable area. With your permission, I will touch your abdomen. If you would rather not, there are other ways to approach the work.

  - I am currently in training as a Zen Shiatsu Specialist at Zen Shiatsu Chicago. Until I complete my program and sit for the MBLEx licensing exam, I may not charge for my services as a Shiatsu bodyworker, though I am insured to work with bodies within my capacity as a student.
  - Each week, I have two one-hour slots open for an exchange. You receive a Shiatsu treatment, and I refine my technique and my practice of Five Element Theory.
