I received my training in somatic education and sexology from Pacific Center of Somatic Sexology, with continued mentorship from Ellen Heed, PhD, and Zen Shiatsu Chicago.

My approach bridges an East-meets-West sensibility, rooted in an eclectic set of contemporary, trauma-conscious somatic methods and ancient ancestral and classical systems of medicine, philosophy, and internal arts. This approach lends a panoramic field for offering deeply integrated and individualized whole-person care options, so that a client gets a direct lived experience of being in their body.

I come into this work from the arts. Transitioning out of the fine art world since 2016 to teach Butoh across Mexico, the US, Germany, and Japan. Butoh emerged in Japan as a somatic practice of resistance and reclamation of the soul following the post-war atomic bomb trauma. It speaks to the truest part of my spirit through its central fulcrum, move to unearth the self. Butoh welcomes what doesn't fit. Otherness, ambiguity, and fluid ways of being gendered and sexual are woven into its stories, with drag and androgyny as natural parts of its language. I find myself through its embrace of otherness.

In time, I gravitated toward Butoh pioneer Minako Seki's evolution of this art form into a more integrated approach to working with the moving body. This begins with how we see with our eyes, how we nourish, and how we care for the body-mind before and after the stage. Happening within a comprehensive holistic lens that integrates meditation, macrobiotic nutrition, Japanese detoxification methods, active imagination, and dance, seeing dance as an expression of living fully. This formation continues to lay the ground for my approach and perspective on health and human potential.

After more than a decade of personal and professional immersion, I am committed to discovering the body as a matrix of wisdom. Where seemingly disparate threads are exquisitely braided into a coherent web. I see that web expressing itself through a person's nervous system, authentic creativity, sexuality, pelvic health, scar tissue, biochemistry, biomechanics, emotional and spiritual landscape, and energetic field.

> "I'm devoted to bridging the gap between what people think they know about their body and what they actually feel, live, and Know as the pulsing wisdom that lives within their cells, organs, and flesh."

At Ma Somatrix, I specialize in supporting people who value self-responsibility, depth, and precision in forging a meaningful relationship with their body. I enable this process through collaborative somatic explorations that teach them how to integrate body literacy, embodied boundaries, and pelvic and ancestral nutrition care practices into their daily lives.

When these skills are embodied in one's voice and heart-mind-body, trust and intimacy are empowered. Out of this space, the soft power of erotic aliveness emerges to reclaim being fully turned on by life.

Allow me to accompany you through this terrain.
