---
title: Two Practices, One Listening
order: 2
---

I move between body-based art and somatic education. These are not separate for me. Art asks
for risk, trust, and vulnerability. Education asks for clarity, pacing, and responsibility.
Both require listening. Both require discipline and play.

Many people who come to my work are highly sensitive, perceptive, and capable. Desire may
feel compelling while difficult to approach without control or shame. Rather than aiming for
release, transformation, or catharsis, I focus on learning how to be in relationship with
sensation, choice, and meaning. Slowly. With curiosity.
