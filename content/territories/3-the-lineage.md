My training is interdisciplinary and lineage-based. I came into this work through the arts,
with early training in psychology and fine art, and am shaped most deeply through sustained
study of Butoh and Noguchi Taiso. These movement lineages teach me how to listen to gravity,
work with subtle force, and let movement emerge through presence.

Most of what taught me this work will never appear on a certificate. A decade of Butoh under
teachers who each carry it differently — Yumiko Yoshioka, Minako Seki, Tadashi Endo, Yuko
Kaseki, Mari Osanai, Natsu Nakajima, Joan Laage, Ken Mai, Diego Piñón, Atsushi Takenouchi,
Michael Sakamoto, Vangeline Theater. Noguchi Taiso, which teaches the body to move as water
moves. Zen Mountain Monastery since 2018, under Jody Hojin Kimmel Sensei.

Named teachers, over years, in rooms. That is where this comes from.

I am certified in Sexological Bodywork and consent-based facilitation, with additional
training in Holistic Pelvic Care™, nervous system–informed embodiment, and Japanese somatic
methods. Currently a student of Chinese Medicine and Zen Shiatsu, where attention to rhythm,
pressure, and relationship is central.
