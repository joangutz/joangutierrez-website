Fundamentally, I am a Zen student. This tradition informs how I embody attention, ethics, and
limits, especially in spaces involving intimacy, power, and vulnerability. I am less
interested in answers than in how one lives the question.

Sessions are personalized to meet the specific needs of clients. They are experiential and
consent-based, shaped by movement, breath, awareness, dialogue, and touch.
