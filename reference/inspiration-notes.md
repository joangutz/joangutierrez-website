## Structure & offer model

### <https://www.dralyssabarba.com>

Love the simplicity and clarity of her practice. Love how focused she is around
individualized monthly care, and that she focuses on women's health from the triad
perspective of digestive, gynecological and emotional health.

**Take:** build my offers within this model of monthly care plans and this model of
coaching. Big fan of the mindful eating plan. Love the way she explains things and the
professionalism and coherence that comes through.

### <https://shannonschultzsomatics.com/>

Elegant, crisp, clean, creative.

Love love love the watercolor imagery and the way her different fields are separated through
that animation flip of the watercolor image. Love the spaciousness. The personal quotes. The
portrait photos. The general way of explaining what to expect from the process, who she
works with, who the work is for, and the forms her work may take — that there may be
crossovers at times and not at others.

**Take:** the structural model for the whole site.

### <https://www.sacredwombservices.com/>

Clean simplicity, compelling.

**Take:** approach session types and pricing the way she does — see
<https://www.sacredwombservices.com/schedule-a-session>

In love with the clarity and coherence of her bio, story and qualifications. Katie Spataro's
bio structure is the model for mine:

> Katie Spataro (she/her) is a somatic sex educator, holistic pelvic care provider and full
> spectrum doula residing on the unceded lands of the Duwamish people otherwise known as
> Seattle. Katie studies and practices at the intersections of birth work and sex work,
> drawing upon the embodied wisdom of these two lineages to support healing of the personal
> and collective cultural body.
>
> Katie blends various modalities of somatic practice, bodywork and skilled coaching in her
> private practice, and facilitates classes on sexual and reproductive wellness, embodied
> consent, grief and loss, pregnancy and birth, and erotic education through a
> trauma-informed and healing-centered lens.
>
> Katie currently serves as core faculty at the Institute for the Study of Somatic Sex
> Education and as adjunct faculty at Antioch University's Sex Therapy Certificate Program.

And the "About Sacred Womb" section as a model for organisational story — origin through
personal experience, lineage, non-ordinary states, embodiment practice, then a short human
close (where she lives, her partner, the garden, the dance floor).

### <https://rememberpleasure.com/>

Thorough professionalism.

Clear outlining of what to expect — time and financial investment as well as the work
itself. What will happen, and an explanation of all the modalities they work with.

**Take:** especially inspired by their professional boundaries and their pages addressing
therapists and pelvic floor physical therapists — educating them about sexological bodywork
and somatic sex education, and how this work can complement and expedite the progress of
their clients.

## Voice & soul

### <https://www.poojaprema.com/>

Love the storytelling style, the poetic prose and mythic language. The symbology of the
collage art is very evocative of beauty and grief, which my work resonates with. My work
centers around the delicate symbol of the flower — its resilience, femininity, perfection,
and cyclical nature.

Love the vibrancy of the font and visual design. The earthiness, reverence, and humanness of
the overall feel. It feels very thoughtfully and lovingly crafted.

**Take:** the About section told as a kind of personal mythology. The total ownership of her
training. The balance between political fierceness and softness. Big fan of the
Roots/Lineage page **instead of** citing certifications and credentials. Like the invitation
to a seasonal newsletter.

**Avoid:** how intellectual and academic it can sound — particularly the Offerings section.
Mine must stay embodied and essential.

### <https://andyburu.se/one-on-one/>

The way he names his process, who he works with, and the themes he works with — power,
surrender, desire — in concrete human portraits.

I recognise my work through those themes, and would name one more: **innocence.**

**Take:** aspire to the mastery of his poetry, philosophy and clarity in what he offers and
how he conveys that approach.

## Visual direction

### <https://selfcervix.com/pages/philosophy>

Of all the sites here, this is the one I align with most in terms of overall design —
elegant simplicity. Bold and soft. Provocative in a subtle way. Confident, embodied. Bare
but full of mystery that leaves you wanting more.

- Colour palette of red and pinks
- Black and white photographic images
- Feminine poetry of the prose
- The fonts, and the empty red void circle symbols
- An entire PHILOSOPHY page, and the concept of the site as a whole
- Focused around feminine initiation through deepening connection to the pelvis
- The simple, personal narrative of her story and what led her to this work

### <https://yoni.com/theyoniversity/>

Like poojaprema, love the feel of collage art. My visual art is very based on collage work,
rooted in similar imagery and symbols — the feminine, earth/nature, transformation,
devotion, the body, the erotic. Really love the added touch of animation on some of the
imagery, and what it evokes.

- Sense of space throughout the pages. I'm overwhelmed by wordiness, and this feels like the
  right amount of necessary information
- Content is closest to mine as a Somatic Sexologist
- Landing page gives a short version with SEE MORE / LEARN MORE — the choice to take in more
- Playfulness of the prose and the personification of the Yoni. An approachable humour to a
  kind of work that can feel intimidating
- Soft, fleshy palette and its different shades

### <https://eileenchao.com/>

Love the natura imagery.

## Condensed summary

- **rememberpleasure.com** — professional thoroughness: clearly outline time + financial
  investment and exactly what to expect.
- **shannonschultzsomatics.com** — the structural model: elegant, crisp, clean, spacious.
  Watercolor imagery separating her fields, personal quotes, portrait photos, and the clear
  way of explaining who the work is for and its forms (which sometimes overlap, sometimes
  don't).
- **sacredwombservices.com** — clean simplicity. Model session types + pricing clarity on
  her Schedule a Session page. Model my bio's clarity and coherence on Katie Spataro's bio
  structure.
- **poojaprema.com** — the SOUL: storytelling, poetic prose, mythic language, collage
  symbology of beauty and grief, earthiness, reverence. Model my About section as personal
  mythology. Use a Roots/Lineage framing INSTEAD of listing certifications. AVOID: her
  Offerings section reads too intellectual/academic — mine must stay embodied and essential.
- **dralyssabarba.com** — structure my offerings as individualized MONTHLY CARE PLANS /
  coaching containers, in her spirit of clarity, education, informing the why for the
  approach, and elegant professionalism.
